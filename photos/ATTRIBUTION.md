# Photo attribution

Everything in `photos/c2/` is cropped out of the Baxtlilar design file itself
(`C2 · Screens`, Figma `oA3c8OF1pLIGQER14CvvFJ`) — the frames were rendered through the prototype view
and the photographs cut out of those renders, so the app shows the pictures the design shows.
No photo depicts a real user of Baxtlilar.

The stock portraits that stood in for member personas were removed together with the screens that
used them.
